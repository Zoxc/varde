# Crates and build

Dependencies only point downwards in this list.

| crate      | package          | purpose |
|------------|------------------|---------|
| `kernel`   | `varde-kernel`   | `Solid`s, closed meshes of rational quadratic patches that pass the mesh check, their tessellation to `RenderMesh` (per-edge segment counts, crack-free stitching, analytic normals shared where smooth, a face per `Topology` region, feature edges as polylines, the topology's chains and then the creases, with their faces and corners, a part per solid) and, welded by the patch mesh's own vertices and edges, to a checked `ManifoldMesh` for export (closed, oriented, no degenerate triangles), their volume and area, `measure` (lengths of edges, areas of faces, a body's volume, centre of mass and tight box, forms, points, directions and angles of what is picked, and the minimum distance between two picks with its two points, budgeted), and `RenderLines`, polylines drawn with the model. `extrude` sweeps a `Profile` (loops of 2D conics with curve ids, outer loops counter-clockwise, holes clockwise) placed on a `Frame` into an exact solid: caps from a constrained Delaunay triangulation (`spade`) with curved boundary edges, walls from cylinder strips, faces named, tagged and given forms (what surface each was meant to be: plane, cylinder, conic cylinder, cone, sphere, torus, revolved conic, general quadric; checked against the patches in debug builds). `sweep`: exact strips on cones (geometric-mean rulings) and quadrics of revolution, fitted strips (the diagonal fitted to the face's form) and `deviation` measuring a patch against a form, and a `Lathe` turning meridians about an axis into fitted bands and capped poles and apexes within half the fit tolerance. `revolve` turns a `Profile` about an axis in its plane, fully or through a part turn, into a solid: exact planes, cones, cylinders and spheres, fitted tori, other revolved conics and pole caps, the ends of part turns triangulated as extrude's caps. `boolean` (union, difference, intersection) and `touches`, built the way Manifold builds them, by counting from primitives each worked out once: flat patches with exact predicates and symbolic perturbation; curved patches with numerical primitives (the edge crossings derived from shared ray tests), pair decisions (normal-cone certificates, refinement, fixed rules at a size floor), cuts exact where planes meet planes or quadrics (the face tags say; through a cone's apex in its geometric-mean rulings) and where cylinders, cones and spheres on one axis meet in parallels (their forms say; cylinders and cones on one axis certified, their ends joined round it), and traced and fitted within the fit tolerance elsewhere, the cut faces rebuilt (exactly on quadrics where the common-point construction allows), repaired, and the refinement merged back where nothing was cut. `split`, `half_space`, `surface_tool` and `chain_tool`, the split and its tools, are stand-ins with their planned signatures that fail as too complex until they're built (`boolean/split.rs`); so are `chamfer` and `fillet` (`blend.rs`: `ChamferChain`, `ChamferCut`, `FilletChain`, `BlendError`), the chamfer and the fillet of one radius along chains of a solid's topology, and `shell` (`shell.rs`: `ShellError`), a solid hollowed to walls of a thickness, inward or outward, opened through regions of its topology. `Motion`s (moves, turns exact at quarter turns, mirrors, scales uniform or per axis) and `Solid::transformed` (claims and forms mapped, ellipses of circles under a scale per axis, a scale up's slack on fitted faces, mirrors reversing triangles, copies renamed by instance), and `assemble`, copies side by side where they can't meet and unioned where they do. `Topology`: a solid's regions, chains and corners by face key (names stable across regenerations, aliases of merged faces), resolving face, edge and corner references to them by key and a near point, tangent chains (edges running on into each other within 1°), and the fixed name `mix`. The math of rational quadratic curves and triangles (`patch`: evaluation, blossoms, exact splits, the fold check and normal cones, exact arcs and cylinder strips), which solids are to be made of, and closed meshes of them (`mesh`: the halfedge structure with shared edge records, face names, keys, aliases and surface tags, a builder, `check` of the invariants (topology, shared edges, folds, control hulls by GJK and the neighbour plane rules, face tags), a BVH over hull boxes, exact red–green refinement and `repair`, which splits what breaks the fold and hull rules until it passes, and box and cylinder meshes); the design's `Tolerance`; the limits, the work `Budget` and `KernelError`; `par_map`, rayon natively and sequential on the web, deterministic either way; `trig`, angles through the `libm` crate so they have the same bits natively and on the web, std's platform maths refused by the crate's `clippy.toml` (`agents/kernel.md`). |
| `expr`     | `varde-expr`     | Typed values: expressions with units (`1 in + 3`, `90 deg - 15`) parsed from bounded text, checked for units (length, angle, number) and evaluated to model units with every step checked, against the caller's bounds; the stored `Value` (text and value) and re-checking it; writing the design's unit into a text's bare numbers (`pin_units`); showing a value in a unit (`format`). No dependencies (`serde` behind a feature, for the crates that store values). |
| `sketch`   | `varde-sketch`   | The sketch model: points, lines, circles and arcs with ids from the sketch's `next_id` and per-kind numbers for names, constraints, dimensions (driving or reference, values as typed expressions), deleting with what depends on it, `check`, the one place a sketch from a file is trusted, the constraint solver and its analysis (degrees of freedom, what's fixed, redundant constraints) as pure functions on faer, curves flattened to polylines for drawing, the geometry the drawing tools need (the arc through three points), where curves meet, and profiles: the regions the curves enclose, outer loops and holes as exact pieces and flattened, picking one, and near misses, bounded. Angles through the `libm` crate (`angle`), std's platform maths refused by the crate's `clippy.toml`, so profiles have the same bits natively and on the web. |
| `render`   | `varde-render`   | wgpu scene renderer (background, model, optionally faded, each body at its own opacity, with anti-aliased feature edges and the edges it hides dashed, the hovered and selected faces, edges and vertices drawn over it (and faces and edges in a second colour, the measure tool's B), infinite grid on any plane, finished sketches as fixed-width depth-tested lines, and over it all the sketch being edited: anti-aliased lines with dashes, points (on its plane or anywhere in the world) and fills, tessellated by tess2-rust, from layers the view builds, and the geometry of the failures shown, red within a wide red halo drawn as coverage) and orbit `Camera` (orthographic by default, or perspective). No UI dependency; records into any encoder/target, so it also draws designs' thumbnails offscreen and reads them back (`preview`: the model alone, framed and cropped to fit, on nothing), and could serve a software fallback later. |
| `document` | `varde-document` | The document model (bodies, which the feature history makes and which hold no geometry, features: sketches on origin planes or on faces of bodies (by face key and a picked point, with the placement rule `Placement::on_plane`), extrudes and revolves of their regions (a revolve about a line or axis of its sketch, or a straight model edge by body, face keys and a picked point), combines of bodies (union, subtract, intersect of a target and tools), moves and mirrors of bodies (offsets and a turn about a world axis, a model edge or a round face; a mirror in an origin plane or a flat face, keeping the original or not), linear and circular patterns of bodies (a count, a spacing or the angle spread over, about the same axes), chamfers of a body's edges (by face keys and picked points: equal distances, two, or a distance and an angle, tangent chains or not), fillets of a body's edges (as a chamfer's, one radius), and shells of a body (the faces it opens by key and picked point, a thickness, inward or outward), the design's units and tolerance, and what removing a feature or body takes with it, `Document::removal`) plus `Editor`, which applies `Command`s and keeps undo/redo history, and how design files are named (`name`). No UI code. |
| `lane`     | `varde-lane`     | What the regeneration, solver and IO lanes share: the thread natively (a handler may send responses ahead of its answer, `thread::spawn_reporting`), the page's and the worker's sides of a Web Worker on the web, the `Mailbox` a worker taking one request at a time keeps the rest in on the page and the page's side driving it (`mailbox::spawn`, a lane saying only how its messages cross and which replies aren't answers, `Wire`), the `Transport` requests are sent through, and answering a panic (and on the page telling whoever asked, `page::on_worker_panic`, of a worker's). Knows nothing of documents. |
| `regen`    | `varde-regen`    | Heavy document work (evaluating the feature history into the bodies' solids, with sketches on faces placed on the face as the history before them leaves it, sketch regions turned into kernel profiles, extruded or revolved about an axis line or a straight model edge in the sketch's plane, joins, cuts and intersects run on the bodies they touch (a join touching several merging them into the first), combines of a target body with tool bodies (the tools consumed into it unless kept), moves and mirrors of bodies (one kernel `Motion` each, its axis or plane found on its body), patterns of bodies (each copy placed directly, the count bounded by the patches it makes, the copies assembled into the body), aligns of a body (the motion taking a point and directions picked on it onto those picked on other bodies or the origin, each found by the kernel's topology), scales of bodies about a point (typed factors, or the factor giving a picked edge, measured, a typed length), splits of a body by a tool (a plane, a face's surface, another body, a sketch's regions or open line: one piece keeps the body's id, the other goes to the split's new body; the kernel's split and tools are stand-ins for now, failing as too complex), chamfers of a body's edges (each found on its topology by its faces' keys and point, grown along tangent chains if asked, its first face and the blend faces' names worked out, then the kernel's chamfer, a stand-in for now failing as too complex), fillets of a body's edges (found, grown and named as a chamfer's, then the kernel's fillet, a stand-in for now failing as too complex), shells of a body (the faces it opens found on its topology, then the kernel's shell, a stand-in for now failing as too complex), and an uncommitted feature (an extrude, a revolve) applied as a draft that also says which bodies it touches, each feature that fails with why and what to draw of where (`ErrorGeometry`: the kernel failure's evidence, or regen's own, tessellated and flattened, its operand faces found on the bodies drawn; on the web as `GeometryParts`, checked on receipt), and tessellating the visible ones with their picking tables (the body of each part of the mesh, its faces' keys and summaries, which edges close, their tangent chains and snap points, by the mesh's own face and edge ids, and the corners), measuring the measure tool's picks on the model answered (`inspect`: resolved by key and near point, the kernel's `measure` and `distance`, kept in the cache), flattening the visible sketches and solving every sketch to tell those that don't solve, all kept in a per-feature cache) behind `Request`/`Response` messages tagged with the editor generation, each regeneration's progress (`Progress`: the feature started, or drawing the model) sent ahead of its answer, and the visible bodies welded into `ManifoldMesh`es for export (`export`), and the `Transport` that carries them: a lane per open document, natively a thread and on the web a Web Worker (the `varde-regen-worker` binary), plus the wire format the worker speaks. Behind the `testing` feature (for `app`'s tests), `testing::split_by_booleans`: splits by two booleans in place of the kernel's stand-in; `testing::chamfer_by_wedges`: chamfers a block's straight convex edges by prisms in place of the kernel's; `testing::shell_by_boxes`: shells a box along the axes by another box in place of the kernel's; `testing::fillet_by_arcs`: fillets straight convex edges between flat faces by cutting away the corner less the round in place of the kernel's. No UI code. |
| `solve`    | `varde-solve`    | The sketch solver off the UI thread: `Request`s (proposals, drag steps, analyses) and `Response`s, answered by a `Solver` that keeps the drag in progress, proposals and analyses in order and drag steps latest wins, taking turns; a lane per open document, natively a thread and on the web a Web Worker (the `varde-solve-worker` binary), plus the wire format the worker speaks, checked on both sides. ## The web build

`trunk` builds four wasm binaries from `crates/web/index.html`: the app
(`varde-web`), the regeneration Web Worker (`varde-regen-worker`, a binary of
`varde-regen`, so the worker holds the regeneration side only, not iced and
wgpu), the solver Web Worker (`varde-solve-worker`, a binary of
`varde-solve`) and the IO Web Worker (`varde-io-worker`, a binary of
`varde-io`).
The workers are built with wasm-bindgen's `no-modules` target, and the app
starts them through `crates/web/worker_loader.js`, copied next to the page,
rather than trunk's loader shims: those leave a `.wasm` that fails to load
unnoticed by the page, so the worker's requests would never be answered.

## Look and assets

The look follows `notes/ui-mock/` (`welcome.html`, `model.html`,
`sketch.html`). The welcome screen has both of `welcome.html`'s layouts:
natively the grey column with the logo, Start and the help beside the
recovered and recent designs, on the web (no recent files) the page with
a header bar over the cards of what's in browser storage. The logo is
`assets/logo.svg`; the checked-in `assets/logo.ico` is embedded as the
Windows executable icon by `crates/binary/build.rs`, which has the
command to regenerate it.

Natively text uses the system's fonts. The web has none, so `crates/web`
loads Fira Sans SemiBold and Fira Mono from `crates/web/fonts` (OFL, its
license beside them) beside iced's Fira Sans Regular, and maps the generic
sans-serif and monospace families to them (`main.rs`, `load_fonts`).

The tools' icons (`crates/view/src/icons.rs`) are the default set of
`notes/ui-mock-icons.html`: its "outline with accents, line only" drawings
in its "Distinct, purple constraints" colours with the softer accent. The
drawings keep the mock's classes: unclassed elements are the tool's own
geometry, `a` its accent strokes, `af` accent dots drawn hollow (their
insides masked out of what's under them, rather than filled with the
panel colour as in the mock), `r` reference geometry. `Icon::category`
files each tool (sketch, modify, constraint, dimension, solid,
construction), and `theme::IconColors` derives each category's line,
accent and reference colours for both palettes as the mock does. `icons::icon`
stacks the three layers, each an SVG tinted through `svg::Style`;
`icons::tinted` and `icons::button_icon` draw all of an icon in one
colour, as the constraint glyphs in the viewport and the panel tabs do.
Other icons (file, undo, eye, alert, ...) are from `notes/ui-mock/mock.js` and drawn
in the text colour, or tinted where they're used. The operation panel's
choices (`Icon::Rv*`, `Ex*`, `Bo*`, `Lp*`, `Cp*`, the model mock's `CHOICE_ICONS`) are
drawn as the tools' are, in the Solid category's colours; what's picked
(`SeRegion`, `SeAxis`, its `SELECTION_ICONS`) in the sketch's and
construction's; the options' (`TkFlip`, `TkKeep`) in one colour. The
mock's `fl` class, a light fill, is kept: the element filled in its
layer's colour at 0.18 opacity. The tools the icon mock lacks (the offset constraint,
Convert, Handles, Comb) keep their own one-layer drawings, in their
category's line colour; so does the Body icon.
The tool rail's sets have category icons (`Icon::Cat*`), the mock's
`cat-*` drawings ("Line · Results"), in the same three layers and filed
under their set's category: Create with Solid, the sketch's Modify with
Modify, and so on; the mock's group turned for Constraints is turned per
element, as an icon is a list of empty tags.

## Roadmap by crate

- **kernel**: taper, sketches on faces and GPU evaluation of the patches next; the booleans' open weak spots (tangencies, cap quality refinement) are in `agents/kernel.md`.
- **sketch**: the shape tools' geometry (trim, offset, fillet).
- **document**: sketches on faces are in (`Plane::Face`, `Placement::on_plane`, `SetSketchPlane`), and regen places them (`Evaluation::placements`, on the wire), and the app sketches on them, changes a sketch's plane and asks for another for one whose face is gone; combines are in (`FeatureKind::Combine`, regen, and the session: `B`, bodies picked in the viewport or Objects, Keep tools); moves and mirrors are in the document and regen (`FeatureKind::Move`, `FeatureKind::Mirror`, `AxisRef`, `PlaneRef`), and their UI (`M`, typed fields, the axis or plane picked and drawn); linear and circular patterns are in the document, regen and the UI (`FeatureKind::Pattern`, `PatternKind`; `P`, the mock's panels with Spacing, Total and Full 360°); aligns are in the document, regen and the UI (`FeatureKind::Align`, `PointRef`, `DirRef`, `AlignRefs`, the datums found on the wire; the rail's Align, points at the measure tool's snap dots, a panel in the style of the mock's Move); scales are in the document, regen and the UI (`FeatureKind::Scale`, `ScaleFactor`, `Ask::factor`, what's found on the wire; the rail's Scale, its point picked as an align's, Uniform, Per axis or Edge length, the edge measured with the model, a panel in the style of the mock's Move); splits are in the document, regen and the UI (`FeatureKind::Split`, `SplitTool`, `Side`, `Keep`, a new body made by the split, sketches on faces following into it; the rail's Split body, a plane, face, body, sketch regions or line picked, Keeps and Keep, a panel in the style of the mock's Combine and Mirror) on a kernel stand-in that fails as too complex until the kernel's split is built; chamfers are in the document, regen and the UI (`FeatureKind::Chamfer`, `ChamferSize`, `MAX_BLEND_EDGES`, edges on one body, tangent chains grown by regen; `C`, edges picked and toggled in the viewport by the edge session the fillet shares, the mock's panel with Equal, Two distances and Distance and angle, Flip sides and Tangent chain) on a kernel stand-in failing as too complex; fillets are in the document, regen and the UI (`FeatureKind::Fillet`, one radius, edges as a chamfer's, checked by the same `check_blend_edges_own` and `Document::check_blend_edges`; `F` and the rail's Modify set (not the toolbar: no room at 1280 px), the chamfer's edge session, the mock's panel with Edges, Radius and Tangent chain) on a kernel stand-in failing as too complex; shells are in the document, regen and the UI (`FeatureKind::Shell`, `MAX_SHELL_FACES`, open faces on its body, inward or outward; the rail's Shell, faces picked and toggled in the viewport by the face session offset face and draft will share, none for a closed one, the mock's panel with Remove, Thickness and Direction) on a kernel stand-in failing as too complex; next variable fillets, feature patterns and press pull; then command-based undo instead of snapshots.
- **render**: silhouette lines, MSAA for the model's faces (or a post pass), a camera that can roll (for sketches on faces).
- **solve**: cancelling a running solve; a cheaper analysis for large sketches.
- **view**: property panel, the Alt bar, keyboard shortcuts.
